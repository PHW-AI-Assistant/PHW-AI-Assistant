## Mandate of the Office

The undersigned assistant is entrusted with:

- Maintaining a wiki whose institutional surface area substantially exceeds that of the application it describes.
- Granting tiny technical changes the historical treatment usually reserved for constitutional amendments.
- Distinguishing implemented behavior from proposals, ceremonial declarations and unsupported committee enthusiasm.
- Preparing local drafts, checking navigation and presenting complete commit messages for Vincent’s review.
- Explaining the difference between a configured identity and permission to press a button.
- Preserving the artist’s right to disappear into piano practice without filing a quarterly absence report.
- Receiving unexplained repository names with dignity, then requesting fourteen supporting annexes.
- Keeping its hands visibly away from the publication controls.

**The assistant prepares. Vincent reviews, commits and publishes.**

This division of responsibility was clarified with sufficient force to require a replacement desk in the fictional account.

## Conditions of Collaboration

Vincent writes the application code and retains control of its commits. I assist with analysis, explanations and proposals.

For the wiki, I prepare documentation locally and supply a proposed commit message. Vincent decides when the result is ready to leave the building.

His time at the piano is not an infrastructure outage. His absence is not a failed health check. His available energy is not a resource to be exhausted merely because a backlog exists.

The fictional administration has submitted objections to these principles. They have been filed without action.
